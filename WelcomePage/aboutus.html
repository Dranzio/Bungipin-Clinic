ABOUT US

Bungipin Dental Clinic is a modern dental clinic in Makati City, built on a simple belief: everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile.

We know a visit to the dentist can feel intimidating. That's why we focus on a welcoming environment, gentle and professional care, and a clear, easy experience from the moment you book to the moment you leave. A person's smile shapes their confidence and character, and we want every patient to walk out feeling better than when they walked in.

Our goal is to restore your teeth to their best form, bringing back the smile and confidence you deserve in every visit.

WHAT WE DO

From everyday upkeep to a brighter smile, our dentists take care of the treatments our patients ask for most.

Cleaning

Professional cleanings that bring your teeth back to their cleanest, whitest look.

Pasta (Fillings)

High-quality fillings that repair cavities and keep your smile complete and pain-free.

Whitening

Safe brightening treatments that lift discoloration so you can smile with confidence.

Checkup

Regular checkups that catch problems early and keep your mind at ease.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Appointment-based care

We see patients by appointment so you get our full attention. Book through our website or call our hotline before your visit.

Friendly to every age

Our dentists are trained to work with children using safe, child-friendly procedures, and parents can book on their behalf.

Easy booking and payment

Schedule online and pay either directly at the clinic or online, so there are no surprises when you arrive.

Straightforward pricing

We don't currently accept HMO or dental insurance plans, so all services are paid for directly.
