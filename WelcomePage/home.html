FAQs

Do you accept HMO or dental insurance?

No, we currently do not accept HMO or dental insurance plans. All treatments and services must be paid directly at the clinic or online.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Unfortunately, walk-in patients are not accommodated. We highly encourage scheduling an appointment through our website or hotline prior to your visit.

Are your services safe for children?

Absolutely! Our dentists are trained to work with pediatric patients using child-friendly, safe procedures.

Can I book appointments for my children?

Yes, parents and guardians can book appointments on behalf of their children through our website or by calling our hotline.
